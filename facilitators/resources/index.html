Resources

Where this comes from.

Nothing here is required reading. The conversation ran for years before anyone at the table had read any of it, and the facilitator's page already holds everything a Wednesday needs. But if you like knowing where an idea came from — or you want more than one page's worth — this is the shelf.

  • Bohm dialogue. The physicist who argued that a real conversation needs a host, not a director — the closest thing we have to a founding theory, even though nobody at the first table had read it.
  • The Circle of Trust. Parker Palmer's rules for rooms where nobody fixes anybody — including the touchstone our fourth principle rhymes with.
  • The Art of Gathering. Priya Parker on why openings and endings deserve better than logistics, and why a light hand still has to be a real one.
  • The working craft. The ordinary mechanics with no famous name attached: silence, quiet voices, and the person who talks too much.
