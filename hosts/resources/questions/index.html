Questions…

A question in the back pocket is insurance, not an agenda — most Wednesdays never need one. But when the table's still quiet at 7:35, it helps to have options. Big questions open a morning. Little questions open a person. Both work.

Big questions

Ours, mostly — pointed at the stories rather than the opinions.

  • What's something you changed your mind about, and what changed it?
  • What did you want to be at seventeen, and how close did you land?
  • What part of your story do you usually leave out?
  • What's a belief you hold that you can't fully defend?
  • What would you do with a completely free year?
  • What does "enough" look like for you?
  • What are you practicing at, these days?
  • What's the best advice you've ignored?
  • When have you been most afraid, and what did you do?
  • What's something your parents got right that took you years to see?
  • What loss has taught you the most?
  • If your life had chapters, what would this one be called?
  • What do you hope people say about you when you're not in the room?
  • What's work for?
  • What's the difference between the person you are and the person people meet?
  • What keeps you up at night that didn't five years ago?
  • What's something you did that you once thought you couldn't?
  • Where do you feel most like yourself?
  • What did your hometown give you?
  • Who changed the course of your life without ever knowing it?
  • When did you first feel like an adult?
  • What rule do you live by that you've never said out loud?
  • What's the most useful thing you've ever unlearned?
  • What are you avoiding right now?
  • What's a door you closed that you still think about?
  • Whose approval are you still working for?
  • What do you miss that you didn't expect to miss?
  • What's a risk you're glad you didn't take?
  • What's the best decision you made for a bad reason?
  • Who taught you how to work?
  • What did your family teach you about money?
  • What's the kindest thing a stranger has done for you?
  • What's a conversation you're still not finished with?
  • When did you last surprise yourself?
  • What's a season of life you wouldn't repeat but wouldn't trade?
  • Who do you still need to thank?
  • What's something hard you chose on purpose?
  • What's the story your family tells about you?
  • What's a compliment you've never forgotten?
  • What are you more sure of than you were ten years ago?
  • What's something you've kept that you should probably let go of?
  • When have you been wrong in a way that turned out well?
  • What's the longest you've stuck with something that wasn't working?
  • Where did your name come from?
  • What did you inherit that isn't a thing?
  • When has quitting been the right call?
  • What's an ordinary day you'd live over exactly as it was?
  • What's the question you hope nobody asks you this morning?

Little questions

From Signal v. Noise's four years of icebreaker data:

  • What was your first job?
  • Have you ever met anyone famous?
  • What are you reading right now?
  • If you could pick up a new skill in an instant, what would it be?
  • Who's someone you really admire?
  • Seen any good movies lately you'd recommend?
  • Got any favorite quotes?
  • Been pleasantly surprised by anything lately?
  • What was your favorite band 10 years ago?
  • What's your earliest memory?
  • Been anywhere recently for the first time?
  • What's your favorite family tradition?
  • Who had the most influence on you growing up?
  • What was the first thing you bought with your own money?
  • What's something you want to do in the next year that you've never done before?
  • Seen anything lately that made you smile?
  • What's your favorite place you've ever visited?
  • Have you had your 15 minutes of fame yet?
  • What's the best advice you've ever heard?
  • How do you like your eggs?
  • Do you have a favorite charity you wish more people knew about?
  • Got any phobias you'd like to break?
  • Have you returned anything you've purchased recently? Why?
  • Do you collect anything?
  • What's your favorite breakfast cereal?

From the xkcd Survey (Randall Munroe):

  • Have you ever been in a plane?
  • Have you ever been skydiving?
  • What's a really popular food that you don't like?
  • When you look at a blue sky, do you see those swirly floaters in your vision?
  • Have you ever had a car run out of gas while you were driving it?
  • What word can you never seem to spell on the first try?
  • Do you eat condiments directly out of the fridge as a snack?
  • When you adjust a thermostat that was set by someone else, it's usually because you want the room to be ...
  • What color is the shirt/dress/upper-body-clothing you're wearing right now, if any?
  • Do you know your Myers-Briggs type?
  • Do you know your astrological sign?
  • Do you feel sleepy a lot?
  • Do you regularly stay awake much later than you meant to?
  • When you think about stuff on the internet, where do you picture it being physically located? Even if you know it's not really how things work, is there a place you imagine websites and social media posts sitting before you look at them? If so, where is it?
  • Can you roll your tongue?
  • Can you pick things up with your toes?
  • How old are you?
  • Do you usually remember your dreams?
  • Do you have strong opinions about text editors?
  • How do you feel about emoji?
  • Does it ever snow where you live?
  • Are you nervous about flying?
  • On a scale of 1 to 5, which number is your favorite?
  • Have you ever thrown out all your different pairs of socks/underwear, bought a bunch of replacements that were all one kind, and then told all your friends how great it was and how they should do it too?
